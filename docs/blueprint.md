# **App Name**: VybzVerse

## Core Features:

- Landing Page: Splash and Landing Page: Animated intro with brand logo/name, scroll-triggered content reveal, tagline, and WhatsApp CTA.
- About Page: About Page: Showcase mission, vision, and story with animated "Why Us?" cards, icons, and testimonials.
- Enquiry Page: Enquiry Page: Direct WhatsApp button with pre-filled message, inquiry form for service requests and partnerships.
- Services Showcase: Services Page: Futuristic cards with icons, short descriptions, and CTAs for Forex Signals, Events, Business Gigs, Knowledge Base, and Partnerships.
- Dynamic Events: Events Module: Timeline-style view for past and future events, including event name, date, poster, and CTA (join, info). Animated carousel for highlights.
- Vybz Moments Gallery: Gallery: Image and video slider of past event moments with optional hover captions and modal viewer.
- Persistent WhatsApp CTA: Floating WhatsApp Button: Persistently visible WhatsApp chat button and CTAs leading to WhatsApp.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Saturated purple (#A020F0) to capture the futuristic and vibrant aesthetic of the platform.
- Background color: Dark grey (#222222) to create a dark-themed base with neon glow effects.
- Accent color: Electric blue (#7DF9FF) as a contrasting analogous color to highlight interactive elements and CTAs.
- Header font: 'Orbitron' (sans-serif) for bold headers with a futuristic, ultra-modern style. Note: currently only Google Fonts are supported.
- Body font: 'PT Sans' (sans-serif)  to complement the headings and to ensure readability.
- Neon-glowing icons to represent each service and event, enhancing the futuristic UI.
- Grid and column-based layout with card-based sections and floating elements to maintain a structured yet dynamic visual experience.
- Smooth entrance animations, interactive hover states, loading transitions, and smooth scrolling to create an immersive feel.
